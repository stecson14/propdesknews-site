Do prop firms actually pay out?

The short answer

Yes, at least the part you can check. Since October 2023, $1.88B in prop firm payouts has been matched to public blockchain transactions, including $124.8M in the last 30 days. These are crypto payouts only, sent through Rise or straight to a wallet. Bank, card and Plane payouts never show up on a blockchain, so treat every number here as a floor, not a total.

How to read this

  • Crypto only. Many firms also pay by bank, so their real totals are higher.
  • Last payout seen is the most useful column. Payouts that stop for days are worth a look.
  • What this can't show: denied payouts, or how long a payout took after the trader asked. For that, read the firm's payout rules and our how to check a firm guide.
  • Pass rates are a different question. See prop firm pass rates.

Questions

Do prop firms actually pay out?

Many do, and some of it can be checked. Payout Junction has matched $1.88B across 1,118,331 payouts from 45 firms to public blockchain transactions since October 2023. That only proves crypto payouts. It says nothing either way about firms that pay by bank.

Why isn't my firm on this list?

It only lists firms whose payouts go through Rise or straight to a crypto wallet, from addresses the firm published or confirmed. Firms that pay only by bank transfer, card or Plane can't show up on a blockchain, so they aren't here. That isn't a sign they don't pay.

Does a bigger total mean a safer firm?

No. A big total mostly means a firm has lots of customers. Look at the last payout date (are payouts still flowing?), the firm's payout rules, and our confirmed rule changes.

Where do these numbers come from?

From Payout Junction, which checks each payout against its blockchain transaction. We refresh them every day and credit them wherever we use them. We also read Tradeify's Rise payout account on Arbitrum ourselves, and our count for the week of Nov 2, 2026 matched theirs to the dollar.

Facts on Prop Desk are read from each firm's own website, help center, X account or email, and every page shows when it was last checked. Prices and rules change often; the firm's own page is the final word. Education, not financial advice.

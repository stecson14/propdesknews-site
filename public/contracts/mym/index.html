MYM futures: tick value, point value and contract specs

Everything a prop trader needs about the Micro E-mini Dow Jones Industrial Average (MYM) future: what a tick and a point are worth, when it trades and expires, and how firms count it against your contract limit.

Edited by Steven T. · Specs from CME Group

The short version

  • Tick value: $0.50. The smallest move is 1.0 index point, worth $0.50 per contract, per CME.
  • Point value: $0.50. MYM is $0.50 x the Dow Jones Industrial Average, and its tick is a full index point, so a tick and a point are the same.
  • 10 MYM = 1 YM in dollar terms. Same index, one tenth the size.
  • Hours: Sunday 6:00 p.m. to Friday 5:00 p.m. ET on CME Globex, with a break from 5:00 to 6:00 p.m. ET each day.
  • Next expiry: the Dec 2026 contract stops trading at 9:30 a.m. ET on Friday, Dec 18, 2026.

Specs are quoted from CME Group's own Micro E-mini Dow Jones Industrial Average contract specs page. Dollar figures are our arithmetic from CME's tick value.

MYM vs YM

MYM and YM track the same Dow Jones Industrial Average and share the same hours, tick size and expiry dates. The only difference is size: YM is $5 a point and MYM is $0.50 a point, so 10 MYM move the same dollars as 1 YM.

A 100-point move is $50 on 1 MYM and $500 on 1 YM. Micros let you size in smaller steps: the same trade idea with a tenth of the dollars per contract.

How prop firms count micros and minis

Futures prop firms cap how many contracts you can hold, with a separate number for minis (NQ, ES) and micros (MNQ, MES). On the 50K accounts below, the micro cap is ten times the mini cap, but TradeDay breaks that pattern on its bigger accounts:

Questions about MYM

What is the tick value of MYM?

One tick is 1.0 index point, worth $0.50 per contract, per CME Group's Micro E-mini Dow Jones Industrial Average contract specs.

How much is one point on MYM worth?

$0.50 per contract. MYM is $0.50 x the Dow Jones Industrial Average, and one tick is a full index point.

How many MYM equal one YM?

10. YM is $5 x the index and MYM is $0.50 x the index, so 10 MYM contracts move the same dollars as 1 YM.

What are MYM trading hours?

On CME Globex, Sunday 6:00 p.m. to Friday 5:00 p.m. ET, with a daily maintenance break from 5:00 to 6:00 p.m. ET.

Prop firms can set stricter cut-off times, so check your firm's rules.

When does MYM expire?

Trading ends at 9:30 a.m. ET on the 3rd Friday of the contract month: March, June, September and December. The next one is Friday, Dec 18, 2026.

How do prop firms count MYM?

Firms set separate caps for minis and micros. At Topstep, Lucid and MFFU the micro cap is ten times the mini cap; Topstep counts 10 micros as 1 mini in the Combine and Express Funded Account. TradeDay caps micros at 50 even on its 100K and 150K accounts.

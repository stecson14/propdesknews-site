How we read the lists

Each list is copied from the firm's own restricted-countries page on Nov 6, 2026 and re-read monthly. Firms word the rule differently: Topstep and Lucid apply it to citizens or residents, Tradeify to residency only, Apex to your ID, residence and bank account together, and Take Profit Trader to any access from a restricted country, including viewing market data. Atlas, Bulenox and Purdia publish no list and refer only to sanctions, so they appear in no count. FTMO Futures and FTMO CFD are listed separately because their lists differ, and The5ers Futures adds Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Zaporizhzhia to the CFD list.

If your country is on a firm's list, the firm's page is the final word and support can confirm exceptions (Topstep and Apex, for example, accept citizens of restricted countries who are permanent residents of eligible ones, with proof). If it isn't, you still face identity, residence and payout-provider checks before money moves; see each firm's KYC answer on its rules page.

Why are some countries restricted?

The firms give four reasons on their pages: sanctions and embargoes, brokers or data providers that cannot serve a region, fraud and chargeback risk, and support or language limits. Apex and Topstep both say their lists can change with partner restrictions.

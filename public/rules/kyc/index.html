When do prop firms ask for KYC?

Every firm that states a rule verifies identity at some point; the difference is when. Take Profit Trader checks at registration, MyFundedFutures before you trade a funded account, and most of the rest before the first payout. Several hand the check to their payout provider, Rise. Firm by firm:

How we checked

Each answer is one of the twelve fixed questions we keep on every firm's rules page, read from the firm's own help center on Nov 6, 2026 and re-read every Monday. We don't rank firms here and we don't infer a rule a firm hasn't written down: where the pages we read are silent, the row says so.

If a firm changes its rule, its rules page and this table change together.

Do prop firms require KYC?

Yes at every firm that states a rule: 17 of the 19 say identity is verified at some stage. Lucid and Bulenox don't say on the pages we read, and Earn2Trade points to onboarding with its partner firm after a funding offer rather than its own check.

No firm in the table says it skips verification.

When does KYC happen: at sign-up, before funding, or before the first payout?

At sign-up: Take Profit Trader verifies every registrant before they become a fully verified customer.

Before you trade a funded account: MyFundedFutures requires it before you begin trading a sim-funded account, and Topstep verifies every trader before Express Funded activation.

After passing: FTMO opens FTMO Identity once you meet the trading objectives (at the first Reward on the Instant Account), FundedNext asks within 30 days of passing, The5ers after a challenge, and Alpha Futures before a Qualified account is issued or any payout.

Before the first payout: Atlas, Tradeify (earlier for residents of ten countries) and FundedNext Futures. Through the payout provider: Funded Futures Family, TradeDay and FXIFY route it through Rise or Riseworks, FXIFY and Tradeify on top of their own check.

What documents do you need for prop firm KYC?

From the firms' own pages: a government photo ID is the common item (Atlas, FundedNext, FundedNext Futures, Elite Trader Funding, Apex for international payout methods, The5ers' passport or national ID). Several add a live selfie or liveness check (Atlas, FundedNext Futures, Elite's biometric check).

The5ers also asks for name, address, date of birth, proof of address and any tax documents required locally; Atlas screens for sanctions and politically exposed persons and records country of residence; Purdia may ask for payment verification and proof that you own the payment method. Payout providers such as Rise run their own document check.

Are there prop firms with no KYC?

Not among these 19. Two firms (Lucid and Bulenox) don't state when or how they verify, which is not the same as not verifying, and every firm that does state it requires verification before a payout at the latest.

A firm that pays you has to know who it is paying; treat any "no KYC" claim elsewhere with that in mind.

What happens if KYC fails or your country is restricted?

FXIFY says it plainly: failing either of its two checks means no funded account and no payouts. Tradeify notes that a Live Account denied at verification can't return to the simulated stage.

Topstep can ask you to re-verify at any time, and Apex can require audits or extra verification at any stage.

If the problem is your country rather than your documents, the firm's restricted-country list applies; our country page shows every firm's list and which firms accept citizens of restricted countries who live elsewhere.

Questions

Do prop firms require KYC before you can trade?

Rarely. Take Profit Trader verifies every registrant at sign-up and MyFundedFutures requires KYC before you begin trading a sim-funded account. Atlas and FundedNext Futures say it is not needed to buy or trade; the check comes before the first payout.

Which prop firms do KYC before the first payout?

Atlas, Tradeify, FundedNext Futures, FundedNext (within 30 days of passing), Alpha Futures (before a Qualified account or any payout), FTMO (after the trading objectives, or at the first Reward on the Instant Account) and The5ers (after passing). Funded Futures Family and TradeDay run the check through the payout provider Rise or Riseworks.

Do payout providers run their own identity check?

Yes at several firms. Tradeify requires its own Sumsub check plus a separate Rise payout verification, FXIFY runs one check with FXIFY and one with Rise, and MyFundedFutures, Funded Futures Family and TradeDay pay through Riseworks, which has its own KYC.

Which firms don't say when KYC happens?

Lucid and Bulenox don't state it on the pages we read. Elite Trader Funding and Purdia say verification is required but not at which stage, and Earn2Trade points to onboarding with its partner firm after a funding offer.

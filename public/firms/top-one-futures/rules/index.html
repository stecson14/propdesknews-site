Top One Futures rules and payouts

Every Top One Futures plan, how each one pays and the rules that can cost you a payout, with every number taken from Top One's own help center.

Edited by Steven T. · Read from Top One Futures' help center Nov 13, 2026

The short version

  • Elite Access: 6% target, no daily loss limit and no consistency rule in the evaluation, pass in 1 day, then an activation fee ($189 on a 50K).
  • Funded Elite Access: 5 profitable days per payout, a 40% consistency rule, a payout buffer and a cap of $1,500 per payout on a 50K, split 90/10.
  • Elite Daily: no activation fee and daily payouts from day 1; the 40% rule applies in the evaluation only.
  • Ignite: instant funding with no evaluation, a 15% consistency rule and payouts at 5% profit.
  • Funded accounts can't trade 2 minutes either side of high-impact news, every trade must last 10 seconds, and nothing is held past 4:10 PM ET.

Top One runs Black November offers in November 2026 (buy one, get one on Elite Access and 25% off activation fees); the fees below are the help center's standard figures.

Drawdown: end of day, static and the buffer

  • Elite Access and Elite Daily use an end-of-day trailing drawdown: it moves up only on your closing balance, never on intraday highs, but the breach level is checked in real time.
  • Elite Access Static keeps the evaluation drawdown fixed. On a 50K the breach level is set at $48,000 and stays there however much you make. Once funded it switches to the end-of-day trailing drawdown.
  • Funded Elite Access drawdowns are $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $3,500 (25K to 150K) and lock at your starting balance plus $100 (Elite Daily: $50,100 on a 50K).
  • Before a payout, funded Elite Access and Elite Daily accounts must hold a payout buffer: $2,000 on an Elite Access 50K, $2,500 on an Elite Daily 50K. You can only withdraw above the buffer plus $500.

Daily loss limit and soft breach

  • Elite Access: no daily loss limit in the evaluation. Funded, a soft breach limit of $500 / $1,000 / $1,250 / $1,750 (25K to 150K) pauses the account for the rest of the day; it does not fail it.
  • Elite Daily: $500 / $1,000 / $1,250 / $1,850 in both the evaluation and funded phases.
  • Ignite: $500 / $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000, also a soft breach.
  • An account can also be paused while a payout request is pending, or until your KYC and funded contract are done.

Consistency rule

  • Elite Access funded: 40%. Your biggest winning day can't be more than 40% of your total profit in the current payout cycle. Top One's example: a $2,000 day needs at least $5,000 of total profit. The count resets after each payout. There is no consistency rule in the Elite Access evaluation.
  • Elite Daily: 40% in the evaluation only, none once funded.
  • Ignite: 15% of total profit per day.
  • Daily Progression Rule (50%) on Elite Access and Elite Daily funded accounts: from the second payout on, at least half of each payout must come from profit made since your last payout.

  • One-phase evaluation: 6% target ($3,000 on a 50K), $2,000 end-of-day drawdown, $1,000 daily loss limit and a 40% consistency rule.

  • No evaluation: you start funded. One-time fee, no monthly or activation charges.
  • 50K: $2,000 end-of-day drawdown, $1,000 soft daily loss limit, 3 minis.
  • Each payout needs 5% profit ($2,500 on a 50K) and a 15% consistency rule; $250 minimum, $1,000 maximum per payout on a 50K.

Why payouts get denied, across firms →

News trading rule

  • Evaluations: news trading is allowed.
  • Funded Elite Access and Elite Daily: no opening, closing or changing orders from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after a high-impact release. You may hold a trade opened before the window, but a stop or target hit inside it counts as a violation.
  • A violation can cost that trade's profit or a payout request; it is not an automatic hard breach unless Top One sees abuse.

Scalping: the 10-second rule

  • Every trade must stay open at least 10 seconds, on all accounts and instruments.
  • A trade closed sooner may not count toward an evaluation, and on funded accounts Top One may remove profits from trades under 10 seconds if they are excessive.

Max contracts and scaling plan

  • Fully scaled limits: 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 minis (10 micros per mini) on 25K to 150K.
  • Elite Access funded accounts start lower and unlock contracts as end-of-day profit grows (a 50K starts at 1 mini). No scaling limits in the evaluation.

Hedging, copy trading and bots

  • Bots and EAs are not allowed. Top One requires manual trading.
  • Copy trading only between accounts of the exact same type and size (an Elite Access 50K to another Elite Access 50K). Mixing types or sizes is a violation.
  • Prohibited practices lead to a breach and forfeited profits.

Inactivity rule and trading hours

  • Trade from 6:00 PM to 4:10 PM ET, Sunday evening to Friday (Friday closes at 4:00 PM). No weekend trading.
  • No overnight holds: open trades are closed automatically at 4:10 PM ET.
  • Place at least one trade every 14 days (336 hours from your last trade) or the account is closed permanently.

Resets and refunds

  • Elite Access evaluation reset: $35, any size. Funded resets: $299 to $1,349 by size.
  • Elite Daily 50K evaluation reset: $82 in the help center ($93 on the homepage on Nov 13); funded reset $499.
  • All purchases are final: Top One has a no-refund policy.

Questions about Top One Futures rules

Does Top One Futures have a consistency rule?

Yes. Elite Access funded accounts use 40% of profit per payout cycle, Elite Daily uses 40% in the evaluation only, and Ignite uses 15%.

Does Top One Futures have daily payouts?

Elite Daily funded accounts can request payouts daily from day 1. Elite Access needs 5 profitable days before each payout.

What is Top One Futures' profit split?

90/10 on every sim funded account.

Can you hold trades overnight at Top One Futures?

No. Open trades are closed automatically at 4:10 PM ET.

What is the Top One Futures 10-second rule?

Every trade must stay open at least 10 seconds. Shorter trades may not count in an evaluation, and excessive ones can lose their profit on a funded account.

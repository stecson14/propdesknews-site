The short version

  • Three monthly evaluations: Zero (no consistency rule, pass in 1 day), Standard (50% consistency, no Daily Loss Guard) and Advanced (40% consistency, higher target, no Daily Loss Guard). No activation fee on any of them.
  • Once Qualified, Zero and Standard use a 40% consistency rule on every payout, Advanced has none, and Direct (no evaluation) uses 20%.
  • Payouts: after every 5 winning days of $200 or more, take up to 50% of your profit, up to a cap ($1,500 Zero, $3,000 Standard, $15,000 Advanced on a 50K). You receive 90%, up to 4 times a month.
  • The Maximum Loss Limit trails at end of day on every account and stops at your starting balance. It doesn't move to your starting balance when you take a payout.
  • On Zero, Standard and Direct Qualified Accounts, no orders within 2 minutes of high-impact news. Advanced Qualified Accounts have no news restriction.

Alpha Futures' own pages differ on whether a day at exactly 40% of your profit passes the Qualified Account rule, so treat 40% as the limit to stay under.

The consistency rules

In one line: Biggest day ÷ net profit, over the whole evaluation and on Qualified Accounts since your last request. Going over never breaches the account.

Alpha Futures divides your biggest day by your total net profit. In an evaluation it is checked over the whole evaluation; on a Qualified Account it is checked on the profit since your last withdrawal request, and it resets after each request.

  • Standard evaluation, 50%: no single day can be larger than 50% of your net profit. Alpha's own suggestion for a 50K with a $3,000 target: keep your best day at $1,500 or less.
  • Advanced evaluation, 40%: the same at 40%. On a 50K with a $4,000 target, Alpha suggests a best day of $1,600 or less, so at least 3 days.
  • Zero and Standard Qualified, 40%: the withdrawal button stays off until your best day since the last request is under 40% of that profit. Going over doesn't breach the account; you keep trading.
  • Direct, 20%: the same rule at 20%.

Alpha's example: on a Zero Qualified Account with a $1,200 best day, you need more than $3,000 of profit since your last request before the rule passes.

Qualified Account payouts

In one line: After every 5 winning days of $200+, take up to 50% of profit up to the cap ($1,500 Zero, $3,000 Standard, $15,000 Advanced on a 50K) at 90%, up to 4 times a month.

PlanMinimumMaximum per request
Zero$200$1,000 (25K) · $1,500 (50K) · $2,500 (100K)
Standard$500$3,000 (50K) · $4,000 (100K) · $5,000 (150K)
Advanced$1,000$15,000 on every size
  • When: after every 5 winning days of $200 or more. They don't have to be in a row, but you need 5 new ones between requests.
  • How much: up to 50% of the profit in the account, or the plan's maximum, whichever comes first. The rest stays in the account.
  • Split: the requested amount leaves your account and you receive 90% of it. Alpha calls the payment a performance fee.
  • How often: up to 4 payouts a month, with no set payout dates. Identity verification (KYC) comes first.
  • The max loss stays put: it doesn't slide to your starting balance at your first payout. If it was at $49,200 before a payout, it stays at $49,200.
  • Methods: ACH (US banks), wire, SWIFT, Wise and Rise, all in USD.

Max loss, Daily Loss Guard and news

In one line: Max loss trails at end of day and stops at your starting balance. The Daily Loss Guard locks you out until 6 PM ET. Most Qualified accounts have a 2-minute news rule.

  • Maximum Loss Limit: end-of-day trailing on every Alpha account; none trail intraday. It follows your highest end-of-day balance and stops at the starting balance. On a 50K Zero, it starts at $48,000 and stops at $50,000 once you close a day at $52,000.
  • It still breaks intraday: touch the limit at any moment, open or closed, and the account is liquidated. On a Qualified Account that closes it.
  • Daily Loss Guard (Zero at both stages, Standard Qualified): hit it and the account locks until the next trading day at 6 PM ET. You don't lose the account. It is $1,000 on a 50K.
  • News: on Zero, Standard and Direct Qualified Accounts, no orders within 2 minutes before or after high-impact news. Breaking it voids that trade's profit and the payout is denied; the first time is a warning. Advanced Qualified Accounts have no news restriction.
  • Inactivity: place a trade at least once every 10 trading days.

Questions about Alpha Futures rules

What is the Alpha Futures consistency rule?

It depends on the plan and stage. Standard evaluations use 50% and Advanced evaluations 40%; Zero evaluations have none. Once Qualified, Zero and Standard use 40% of the profit since your last request, Direct uses 20%, and Advanced has no consistency rule.

How do Alpha Futures payouts work?

On Zero, Standard and Advanced Qualified Accounts, you can request up to 50% of your profit after every 5 winning days of $200 or more, up to the plan's cap. You receive 90% of each request, up to 4 times a month.

How fast can I pass an Alpha Futures evaluation?

Zero can be passed in 1 day because it has no consistency rule. Standard needs at least 2 days and Advanced at least 3.

Does Alpha Futures charge an activation fee?

No. Zero, Standard and Advanced have no activation fee. The evaluations are monthly subscriptions.

Can I trade news at Alpha Futures?

Not within 2 minutes before or after high-impact news on Zero, Standard and Direct Qualified Accounts. Advanced Qualified Accounts have no news trading restriction.

Is the Alpha Futures drawdown end of day?

Yes. Alpha says the Maximum Loss Limit trails at end of day on all of its accounts and stops at the starting balance. Touching it at any moment still breaches the account.

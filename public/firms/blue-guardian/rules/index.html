The short version

  • Funded futures traders get a 90% profit split, and every payout carries a 3% processing fee.
  • Consistency depends on the plan: Elite has 40% on funded accounts only; Reserve has 50% (or an optional 40%) on the challenge only; Direct scales from 20% to 25% to 30% across your first three payouts.
  • Blue Guardian Futures charges no monthly fee and no activation fee; you pay once at checkout.
  • Going over a consistency rule doesn't breach the account. The payout button stays off until your best day falls under the line.
  • Payouts go by Rise or crypto, processed within 24 business hours. Minimum $500 on Elite, Reserve and Direct.

Blue Guardian also sells CFD/forex accounts with different rules. This page covers the futures accounts only.

Blue Guardian Futures consistency rule by plan

In one line: Elite: 40% on funded accounts only. Reserve: 50% (or 40%) in the challenge only. Direct: 20%, 25%, then 30%. Going over never fails the account.

Blue Guardian Futures consistency rule by plan
PlanConsistencyWhen it applies
Elite40%Funded accounts only, per payout cycle
Reserve50%, or 40% as a cheaper optionChallenge only; funded Reserve accounts have none
Direct20%, then 25%, then 30%1st payout, 2nd payout, 3rd payout onward

Worked example (Elite, 40%). A $2,000 best day needs at least $5,000 of total profit before you can request a payout. On Direct's first payout (20%), a $1,000 day needs $5,000.

Reserve cushion. The 50% Reserve option gets a 1% tolerance: on a $50K Reserve account with a $3,000 target, the largest day can be up to $1,530 instead of $1,500.

Breaking the rule never fails the account; you keep trading until your best day is back under the percentage.

Reserve account rules (25K to 150K)

In one line: 50K: $3,000 target, $2,000 end-of-day drawdown, no daily loss limit unless you add one, news allowed, and payouts up to 50% of profit per request.

Payouts are capped at 50% of your profits per request, up to the fixed cap for your size.

Drawdown lock on funded accounts

In one line: End-of-day drawdown that locks at start + $100 on funded accounts ($50,100 on a 50K), and after any approved payout.

Blue Guardian Futures uses an end-of-day (EOD) drawdown. On funded accounts it stops trailing at your starting balance + $100: on a 50K, once you close a day at $52,100, the floor locks at $50,100.

After any approved payout it locks there anyway, on every futures plan: $25,100, $50,100, $100,100 or $150,100. That stops you withdrawing down to the exact breach level.

Payout split, fee, minimums and speed

In one line: 90% split with a 3% processing fee on every payout, and a $500 minimum on Elite, Reserve and Direct. Rise or crypto within 24 business hours.

  • Split: 90% to the trader on all funded accounts.
  • Fee: a 3% processing fee on every payout.
  • Minimums: $500 on Elite, Reserve and Direct; Express is $250 on 25K and 50K, $500 on 100K and 150K.
  • Methods and speed: Rise or crypto, processed within 24 business hours. Crypto payouts max out at $1,000 per request; larger ones go through Rise.

Micro-scalping rule

In one line: Scalping is allowed, but under 50% of your profit may come from trades held less than 10 seconds.

Scalping is allowed, but less than 50% of your total profits may come from trades held under 10 seconds. Relying on ultra-short trades can lead to profit removal or a payout review.

Questions about Blue Guardian rules

What is the Blue Guardian Futures consistency rule?

It depends on the plan. Elite: 40% on funded accounts only. Reserve: 50% (or an optional 40%) on the challenge only.

Direct: 20% on the first payout, 25% on the second and 30% from the third. Going over doesn't breach the account; you just can't request a payout or pass until your best day is back under the line.

Does Blue Guardian Futures have an activation fee?

No. Blue Guardian says its futures accounts have no monthly subscription and no activation fee; you pay once at checkout.

What is Blue Guardian's profit split on futures?

90% to the trader on all funded futures accounts, and each payout carries a 3% processing fee.

How fast does Blue Guardian pay?

Within 24 business hours, by Rise or crypto. The minimum is $500 on Elite, Reserve and Direct.

Is Blue Guardian Futures the same as Blue Guardian CFD?

They're sold by the same firm, but the CFD accounts have different rules. This page covers the futures accounts only.

Keep up to 100% of sim profit up to $25,000, paid twice a week, with strict Active Trade Day rules.

Pick it if

  • You want the biggest share: up to 100% of sim profit up to $25,000.
  • You want regular paydays: rewards are paid on Mondays and Wednesdays.
  • You want an end-of-day drawdown: the EOD plan trails your highest end-of-day balance.

Skip it if

  • You let winners breathe on 1-Step. Its drawdown trails your highest unrealized profit.
  • You trade small days. Rewards need 8, then 10 Active Trade Days of $250+ that are at least 30% of your best day.
  • You hold overnight or skip weeks. No overnight holds and at least one trade every week.

Funded Trader Markets (FTM) rules and payouts

Every Funded Trader Markets plan, how each one pays and the fine print that can void a reward, with every number taken from FTM's own FAQ.

Edited by Steven T. · Read from Funded Trader Markets' help center Nov 13, 2026

The short version

  • 1-Step Nitro: 10% target, 4% daily loss, 6% trailing max loss, and 90% on the first $10,000 of profit.
  • Consistency: 45% on Nitro funded and 15% on Instant; none on 2-Step Plus or Infinity.
  • 2-Step Plus and Instant Plus: keep floating loss under 1% or the split drops.
  • News trading, EAs, overnight and weekend holds are allowed, and every account is swap-free.
  • Profit from trades closed in under 1 minute is voided on funded accounts.

Funded Trader Markets is a CFD firm on cTrader, TradeLocker, Match-Trader and MT5. Its 24/7 perpetuals desk, FTM Perps, has its own rules on the FTM hub.

1-Step Nitro rules

  • One phase with a 10% profit target and no time limit (Nitro target ↗).
  • Daily loss is 4% of the starting balance, or 3% on the 300K (Nitro daily drawdown ↗).
  • Max loss is 6% trailing from your highest balance. Once you are up 6%, it locks at your starting balance (Nitro overall drawdown ↗).
  • Consistency applies in both phases: in the evaluation no day may make more than half the target, and funded no day may be more than 45% of total profit (Nitro consistency ↗).
  • No minimum days to pass. Funded, you need 5 days of at least 0.5% profit before a reward (Minimum days ↗).
  • Split is 90% on the first $10,000 of profit, then 80%. Free accounts from giveaways get 80% (Nitro split ↗). Max allocation is $300,000 (Nitro allocation ↗).
  • A reward taken below 6% profit resets your balance, so the trailing floor sits closer: on a 100K with a $105,000 high, a full $4,000 reward leaves $1,000 of room (Nitro drawdown and rewards ↗).

1-Step Infinity and Infinity Plus

  • Both share one evaluation: a 9% target, 3% daily loss, 3% static max loss, no consistency rule and no minimum days (Infinity vs Infinity Plus ↗).
  • Infinity rewards start at $50, with a per-request cap of $250 on a 5K up to $1,000 on a 40K, and a 2% balance buffer after each reward (Infinity reward rules ↗).
  • Infinity Plus drops the cap and the buffer, but only goes up to $20,000 per account (Infinity Plus add-on ↗).
  • After the first approved reward, the max loss floor moves up to the starting balance (Infinity overall drawdown ↗).
  • Allocation is $40,000 on Infinity, plus $20,000 on Infinity Plus, $60,000 combined (Infinity allocation ↗).

2-Step Plus rules

  • Targets are 8% in Phase 1 and 5% in Phase 2 (2-Step Plus targets ↗), with 4% daily loss and a 10% static max loss (2-Step Plus drawdown ↗).
  • No consistency rule in either phase or funded (2-Step Plus consistency ↗). Each phase needs 3 trading days; funded needs 5 days of 0.5% profit (Minimum days ↗).
  • Split is 70/30 while you follow the Shield Risk Protocol: floating loss may not reach 1% of the starting balance. Each breach closes your trades and cuts the split, to 50/50, then 40/60, 30/70 and finally 20/80 for good (Shield Risk Protocol ↗).
  • A free add-on raises the split to 90/10, but the Shield rule then applies in the evaluation too, and a second breach fails it (90% add-on ↗).

Instant funding rules

  • No evaluation. All three Instant accounts have a 3% daily loss, a 15% consistency rule and need 5 days of 0.5% profit before a reward (Instant consistency ↗).
  • Standard: 5% trailing max loss, split 50%, 60%, 70%, then 80% from the 4th reward, up to $200,000 (Instant Standard split ↗).
  • Pro: 3% trailing max loss that never locks, same rising split, up to $100,000 (Instant Pro drawdown ↗).
  • Plus: 6% trailing max loss that locks once you are up 6%, and 80/20 from the start while floating loss stays under 1% (Instant Plus floating loss ↗).

Drawdown: trailing or static

  • Trailing: 1-Step Nitro (6%), Instant Standard (5%), Instant Pro (3%) and Instant Plus (6%) follow your highest balance. Nitro and Instant Plus stop trailing once you are up 6% (Instant Standard drawdown ↗).
  • Static: 2-Step Plus (10%) and 1-Step Infinity (3%) stay at the starting balance minus the limit.
  • Daily loss resets at 5 PM ET from the higher of balance or equity, so open profit at the close raises the floor for the next day (Daily drawdown example ↗).

Consistency rule

  • Best day % = best day profit ÷ total profit × 100, with a day counted from 5 PM ET to 5 PM ET (Nitro consistency ↗).
  • Nitro evaluation: no day over 50% of the profit target. Nitro funded: 45% of total profit. Instant: 15%. 2-Step Plus and Infinity: none.
  • Go over and nothing breaches: you keep trading until your best day is back under the line.

News trading rule

  • News trading is allowed in every program (News trading ↗).
  • News scalping, opening and closing within seconds around high-impact releases, is banned, as are news-scalping bots (Trading bots ↗).

IP rule and copy trading

  • Your trading IP may not match another FTM user's. No VPN or VPS for buying or KYC, and the purchase country must match your KYC country (IP rules ↗).
  • MT5 and cTrader can't be traded from a US IP, even through a VPN. Traders in one household must tell FTM first and share one allocation cap.
  • Copiers are allowed for your own trades, including from another firm or broker. Copying other traders or signals is not (Trade copiers ↗).
  • FTM has its own copier in the dashboard (FTM copier ↗), not available on TradeLocker (TradeLocker copy trading ↗).
  • Same pair on two accounts: open both within 5 minutes, or close the first before opening the second (Sequential trading ↗).

EAs and bots

  • Bots are allowed on all accounts and phases (Trading bots ↗).
  • Banned types: high-frequency, news scalping, demo-server exploits, multi-account reverse trading, reverse and latency arbitrage, tick scalping and emulators. Using one gets the account closed with no refund.
  • Running the same bot as another FTM trader counts as copying and is not allowed (Trade copiers ↗).

Minimum trading days

  • 1-Step Nitro: none to pass, 5 funded days before a reward.
  • 1-Step Infinity: none in either phase.
  • 2-Step Plus: 3 per phase, then 5 funded.
  • Instant: 5 before a reward.
  • Only days with at least 0.5% profit count, and there is no maximum time limit (Minimum days ↗).

Swap-free and weekend holding

  • Every account is swap-free at no extra charge (Swap charges ↗).
  • Overnight and weekend holding is allowed in all programs (Overnight and weekend ↗), and crypto trades on weekends.
  • Commission is $7 per lot round turn on forex and commodities, none on indices and crypto (Commission ↗).

Payouts: minimum, methods and timing

  • Rewards are on demand once you meet the plan's rules; the minimum is a profit of 1% of the starting balance (Minimum reward ↗).
  • Paid in USDT (TRC20) or through Rise, by crypto or bank transfer. Crypto rewards carry a 5% processing fee from your share (Reward methods ↗).
  • Close all trades before you request (Close trades first ↗).
  • Rewards up to $1,000 not paid within 24 business hours (8 AM to 5 PM ET, Monday to Friday) are doubled, plus a free account of the same size (24-hour guarantee ↗).
  • No refunds, but the 3rd reward includes a bonus equal to your fee, except on Instant and Infinity (Fee bonus ↗).

Hidden rules: the fine print to read first

  • 1-minute trades: funded profit from trades closed in under a minute is voided. In a challenge, if over 20% of profit comes from them, you redo it (Minimum holding time ↗).
  • Rollover gaps: opening in the 5 minutes before rollover and closing in the 5 after makes that profit ineligible (Gap trading ↗).
  • Hedging only within one account, never across accounts (Hedging ↗).
  • Martingale and layering are fine in one account but not spread across accounts (Martingale ↗).
  • Margin: new trades are blocked at a 110% margin level and positions close at 100%, without breaching the account (Margin rule ↗).
  • Rise name must match your FTM name, and a wallet or email shared with another user can forfeit the reward (Reward methods ↗).
  • 30 days with no activity can end the account unless you tell FTM first (Inactivity ↗).
  • KYC: a funded account stays read-only until KYC is done; a driver's license only works for US, UK, Australian or Canadian residents (KYC documents ↗).

Questions about Funded Trader Markets rules

Does Funded Trader Markets have a consistency rule?

1-Step Nitro: no day over 50% of the target in the evaluation and 45% of profit funded. Instant accounts: 15%. 2-Step Plus and 1-Step Infinity: none.

Is the FTM drawdown trailing?

On 1-Step Nitro and the Instant accounts, yes. Nitro and Instant Plus lock once you are up 6%. 2-Step Plus and Infinity are static.

Can you trade the news at Funded Trader Markets?

Yes, in every program. Only news scalping within seconds of a release is banned.

Can US traders use MT5 at FTM?

No. FTM does not allow US IPs on MT5 or cTrader, even through a VPN.

What is the Funded Trader Markets profit split?

90% on the first $10,000 on 1-Step Nitro, then 80%. 2-Step Plus starts at 70/30 (90/10 with the free add-on), Instant at 50% rising to 80%.

Funded Futures Family rules and payouts

The five plans Funded Futures Family sells to everyone, how each one's drawdown works, the consistency rules that gate payouts and how much each payout can be, from FFF's own help center and site.

By Prop Desk Staff · Read from Funded Futures Family's help center Nov 4, 2026

The short version

  • Five open plans: Prime and Choice (end-of-day drawdown evaluations), Velocity (intraday trailing evaluation), and two no-evaluation 50K accounts, S2F Payday and S2F Accelerate. No activation fee on any plan, and every payout is split 90/10.
  • Fastest passes: Prime can pass in 1 day with no evaluation consistency rule. Choice needs 3 days (40% consistency), or 2 with a 50% add-on. Velocity needs 3 days and keeps a 40% rule for the life of the account.
  • Prime, Choice and Payday drawdowns trail at end of day and lock near the starting balance. Velocity and Accelerate trail intraday, counting open profit. FFF says a payout doesn't lower the Prime or Velocity drawdown, and Choice locks its floor at your first payout, so payouts eat into your cushion.
  • Payout gates differ: Prime needs 3 days and a profit target between payouts, Choice Weekly needs 5 winning days of $200+, Velocity 3 qualifying days, Payday 7, Accelerate 5. Choice Daily Pay and the Velocity Daily add-on allow one request a day.
  • S2F Accelerate accounts bought on or after October 30, 2026 pay a flat $500 per payout after $1,500 of new profit, with a 20%, 25%, then 30% consistency rule.

Premier+, Sniper and a 25K S2F Accelerate are invite-only, so they aren't covered here. S2F Accelerate rules depend on when the account was bought; older accounts keep the rules they were bought under.

S2F plans start funded, with no evaluation. *Removed on the funded account by Velocity's Daily Payout add-on.

  • Sizes: Prime, Choice and Velocity come in 25K, 50K, 100K and 150K. Payday and Accelerate are 50K only.
  • Daily loss limits: none in the Prime or Choice evaluation, on Prime or Choice Weekly funded accounts, or on S2F Accelerate. S2F Payday has a $1,000 hard daily loss limit, and Choice Daily Pay has one too (see payouts).
  • After you pass Prime or Velocity, you have 7 days to activate the funded account or it closes. There is no activation fee.
  • Choice is billed monthly: FFF says the Choice evaluation is a subscription that rebills each month, and a reset costs $5 less than the evaluation.
  • Blown funded account: FFF offers a Funded Reset, up to three times per account.

Profit target / max drawdown, from FFF's evaluation articles.

  • Prime: pass in as little as one trading day. No consistency rule and no daily loss limit in the evaluation.
  • Choice: your best day can be no more than 40% of total profit, so three days is the earliest pass. A checkout add-on (20% more on the price) makes it 50%, so two days. FFF's example: a $1,500 best day needs at least $3,750 of total profit. Passing is checked at the end of each day. Neither rule carries into the funded account.
  • Velocity: at least 3 trading days and a 40% consistency rule that stays for the life of the account. FFF says the Daily Payout add-on removes it on the funded account.
  • News: FFF's Choice article says news trading is allowed, with its standard rules on trading hours, inactivity and prohibited conduct.

How each drawdown works

  • End of day (Prime, Choice, Payday): the floor is your highest closing balance minus the drawdown, recalculated after each session, so intraday swings don't move it. It still breaks the moment your balance touches it.
  • Where it locks: Prime locks at the starting balance once you close above the starting balance plus the drawdown ($52,000 on a 50K). Payday locks at $50,000 after a $52,000 close. Choice funded accounts lock at the starting balance plus $100, either when you close at starting balance plus drawdown plus $100 or when you take a payout.
  • Intraday (Velocity, Accelerate): the floor follows your highest account value including open profit, in real time. A trade that runs up and comes back still drags the floor up. FFF's Velocity article doesn't describe a lock.
  • After a payout: FFF's Prime and Velocity articles say the drawdown doesn't reset or move down, and a Choice floor locks when you take a payout. Either way, a payout shrinks your cushion.
  • Choice Daily Pay uses a tighter funded drawdown on bigger accounts: $2,500 on 100K and $3,500 on 150K, against $3,000 and $4,500 in the evaluation.

Consistency rules by plan

  • Prime funded: no single day can be more than 40% of your profit in that payout cycle. It resets after each payout.
  • Velocity: 40% for the life of the account, evaluation and funded. The Daily Payout add-on removes it on the funded account.
  • S2F Payday: 40%.
  • S2F Accelerate (bought on or after October 22, 2026): 20% for payout 1, 25% for payout 2 and 30% after that. Accounts bought before then keep 25% for life.
  • Choice: 40% (or 50% with the add-on) in the evaluation only. No funded consistency rule.

A qualifying or winning day is a day with at least $200 of profit. Caps are before the 90/10 split.

  • Other sizes: Prime caps run $1,000 / $1,500 (25K) up to $3,500 / $4,000 (150K). Choice Weekly caps are $1,250, $2,500, $3,500 and $4,500 by size, the same every payout. Velocity caps are $750, $1,250, $2,250 and $3,250 (Daily add-on $600, $1,000, $1,500, $2,500).
  • Choice Weekly: request up to 50% of total profit (balance minus starting balance), minimum $500. FFF's example: at $54,000 on a 50K you can take $2,000 and receive $1,800.
  • Choice Daily Pay: one request per trading day after the 2 PM Pacific close, minimum $250, and the balance must stay above the cushion ($52,100 on a 50K). It has a daily loss limit ($1,000 on a 50K): the first hit stops trading for the day, the second closes the account, and strikes never reset.
  • Between payouts on Choice you need at least $1 of net profit since the last payout. Only one request can be pending at a time.
  • S2F Accelerate accounts bought before October 30, 2026 keep higher caps ($1,250, then $1,500). The minimum payout is $500, so newer accounts pay exactly $500 each time.
  • Getting paid: FFF says requests go into review when you submit them and most are reviewed the same day. Approved payouts go through Rise, usually within 24 hours; bank transfers can take 1 to 3 business days.

Questions about Funded Futures Family rules

Does Funded Futures Family have a consistency rule?

It depends on the plan. Prime has none in the evaluation and 40% per payout cycle once funded. Choice has 40% (or 50% with an add-on) in the evaluation only. Velocity has 40% for the life of the account, removed on the funded account by the Daily Payout add-on. S2F Payday uses 40% and S2F Accelerate 20%, 25%, then 30%.

How fast can I pass a Funded Futures Family evaluation?

Prime can be passed in one trading day. Choice needs at least three days because of its 40% consistency rule, or two with the 50% add-on. Velocity needs at least three trading days.

Is the Funded Futures Family drawdown end of day?

On Prime, Choice and S2F Payday, yes: it trails your highest closing balance and locks near the starting balance. Velocity and S2F Accelerate use an intraday trailing drawdown that counts open profit. FFF says the Prime and Velocity drawdowns don't reset after a payout.

What is the Funded Futures Family profit split?

90/10 on every plan: you keep 90% of each approved payout. Payout caps and minimums are stated before the split.

Does Funded Futures Family charge an activation fee?

No. FFF says there is no activation fee on any plan.

Does Funded Futures Family offer daily payouts?

Yes on Choice Daily Pay, one request per trading day above a payout cushion, and with Velocity's Daily Payout add-on, one request per day after meeting the profit requirement.

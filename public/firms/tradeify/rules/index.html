Tradeify rules and payouts

The Select evaluation, the consistency rule, and how each Tradeify funded account pays out, with every number taken from Tradeify's own help center.

By Prop Desk Staff · Read from Tradeify's help center Nov 3, 2026

The short version

  • Every Tradeify funded account pays a 90/10 split: Select Flex, Select Daily, Growth and Lightning.
  • The Select evaluation has a 40% consistency rule (50% with a paid add-on), a 3-day minimum (2 with the add-on), end-of-day drawdown and no daily loss limit. Once you're funded on Select, there's no consistency rule.
  • After passing Select you pick Flex (a payout every 5 winning days, up to 50% of your profit) or Daily (a payout any day, up to 2× what you made since the last one, above a buffer). The choice is permanent for that account.
  • Growth funded payouts use a 35% consistency rule. Lightning (instant funded) uses 20%, then 25%, then 30% from the third payout.
  • Drawdown is end of day and locks at $100 above your starting balance once your balance clears it (e.g. $50,100 on a 50K).

Payout caps shown are for accounts bought on or after Sep 1, 2026; older caps are listed under each table. The 300K Select account has its own rules and isn't covered here.

The consistency rule

Your biggest day ÷ your total profit must be at or below your account's limit. Exactly 40.00% meets a 40% rule; only a figure above it fails. Commissions aren't counted as profit. It never stops you trading: a big day just means you need more total profit before you can pass or request a payout.

Tradeify's example (Select 50K, $3,000 target): a $1,300 day needs $3,250 in total profit ($1,300 ÷ 0.40).

Questions about Tradeify rules

What is Tradeify's consistency rule?

Your biggest day must be at or below a share of your total profit: 40% in the Select evaluation (50% with the add-on), 35% for Growth funded payouts, and 20%, 25%, then 30% for Lightning funded payouts. Select funded accounts have no consistency rule.

Does Tradeify have a consistency rule on funded accounts?

Not on Select funded accounts, whether you chose Flex or Daily. Growth funded accounts use 35% and Lightning funded accounts use 20% to 30%, checked at each payout.

What is Tradeify's profit split?

90% to you and 10% to Tradeify on every funded account type.

What's the difference between Select Flex and Select Daily?

Flex pays every 5 winning days, up to 50% of your total profit, with no daily loss limit. Daily lets you request any day, up to 2 times the profit you made since your last payout, but adds a daily loss limit and a buffer. You choose after passing and can't switch for that account.

What is the maximum payout on a Tradeify 50K Select account?

Flex: up to 50% of your total profit, capped at $2,500 per payout for accounts bought on or after Sep 1, 2026 ($3,000 if bought before, $2,000 with the 50% consistency add-on). Daily: up to $1,250 per payout ($1,000 if bought before Sep 1, 2026).

What is Tradeify's minimum payout?

$250 on Select Flex and Select Daily. Growth: $250, $500, $1,000 or $1,500 by account size. Lightning: $1,000.

When does Tradeify's drawdown stop trailing?

When your end-of-day balance clears the trigger (for example $52,100 on a 50K), the floor locks at $100 above your starting balance and never moves again. On Select Flex it also locks when you request a payout.

How fast does Tradeify pay?

Usually within 24 hours of approval once your Rise onboarding is complete, and up to 72 hours for requests outside business hours or over a weekend.

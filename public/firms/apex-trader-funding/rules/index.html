The short version

  • Approved payouts from a Performance Account (PA) are paid at a 100% split. A PA is simulated funded, not a live account.
  • You need 5 qualifying days, each with at least the minimum daily profit for your size: $100 to $350 on EOD accounts, $100 to $300 on Intraday accounts.
  • 50% consistency: no single day can be 50% or more of your profit since your last payout. It applies in the PA, and Apex lists it as not applied in the EOD evaluation.
  • The minimum payout is $500, and your balance has to stay above the safety net (your drawdown plus $100) for the life of the account.
  • Each PA allows 6 payouts, each with its own cap. After the sixth, the PA closes.

These are the rules for Apex's current EOD and Intraday accounts. Legacy accounts, which Apex brought back for a limited time on Nov 1, follow older rules that aren't covered here.

What you need before you can request a payout

In one line: 5 qualifying days ($250+ on an EOD 50K, $200+ on Intraday) and a balance of at least $52,600 on a 50K before you can request.

You need 5 qualifying days. They don't have to be in a row, and there's no deadline, so payouts can come as often as weekly. The 50% consistency rule

In one line: Your biggest day must be under 50% of your net profit since the last payout. Going over only hides the payout button.

Your biggest profitable day has to be less than 50% of your net profit since your last approved payout (or since the account started). To find the profit you need, take highest day ÷ 0.5.

  • Apex's example: a $1,500 best day on a 50K PA means you need $3,000 of net profit.
  • Losing days count, because it uses net profit. Make $1,000 on day 1 and lose $200 on day 2, and your best day is 125% of profit.
  • Going over 50% doesn't fail the account. The payout button just doesn't appear until you get back under.
  • It resets after each approved payout.

One wrinkle: Apex's rule text says "50% or more" fails, but the example table on the same page marks exactly 50% as met. Leave yourself a little room.

Evaluation and account rules

In one line: 50K: $3,000 target, $2,000 max drawdown, a $1,000 evaluation daily loss limit that pauses trading, 30 days to pass and 7 days to activate the PA.

Figures for EOD accounts. The max drawdown is the same on Intraday accounts.

  • EOD evaluation: 30 days to hit the target, no minimum number of days, so you can pass in one day. Consistency and scaling aren't applied. After you pass, you have 7 days to activate the PA.
  • EOD drawdown is set once a day at the close and enforced during the next session. Touch it at any time and the account is liquidated.
  • Intraday drawdown follows your peak balance in real time, including open profit. In a PA it stops trailing once it reaches your starting balance plus $100.
  • Hitting the daily loss limit pauses trading for the session. It doesn't close the account.
  • You can hold up to 20 PAs at once.

Apex activation fee for the Performance Account

In one line: A one-time, non-refundable fee after you pass, paid within 7 days. No Activation Fee evaluations charge $0.

Apex charges a one-time PA activation fee after you pass an Evaluation. It is not a subscription and doesn't renew, and it can't be refunded, moved to another account or reversed.

  • 7-day window. You have 7 calendar days from when the Evaluation is marked Passed (after the 6 PM ET reopen) to pay it with the Purchase PA link. Miss it and you have to pass a new Evaluation.
  • No Activation Fee Evaluations. Apex also sells evaluations where the PA fee is $0 at checkout; the rules, drawdown and access period are the same.
  • Lose the PA, pay again. If a PA is disqualified, liquidated or closed, you need a new Evaluation and a new activation fee.

The fee amount depends on the account and platform; see the current numbers on the Apex profile and on Apex's checkout.

Questions about Apex rules

What are Apex's payout rules?

You need 5 qualifying days that each meet the minimum daily profit for your account size, a balance above the safety net plus the $500 minimum payout, and no single day at 50% or more of your profit since your last payout. Approved payouts are paid at a 100% split, with up to 6 payouts per Performance Account.

What is Apex's consistency rule?

No single profitable day can be 50% or more of your net profit since your last approved payout. Divide your best day by 0.5 to get the net profit you need. Going over doesn't fail the account; you just can't request a payout until you're back under.

Does the Apex consistency rule apply in the evaluation?

Apex's EOD Evaluations page lists consistency as not applied. The 50% rule applies when you request payouts from a Performance Account.

How many payouts can I take from one Apex account?

Six. Each payout has its own cap. On a 50K EOD account, for example, they run from $1,500 to $3,000, for $13,000 in total. After the sixth payout the account closes.

What is the Apex safety net?

Your drawdown limit plus $100, for example $52,100 on a 50K account. Your balance has to stay above it for the life of the Performance Account, and only profit above it can be paid out.

What is Apex's minimum payout?

$500 per request, on every account size.

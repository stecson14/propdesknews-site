MyFundedFutures (MFFU) rules and payouts

How each MyFundedFutures plan is passed and paid, with every number taken from MFFU's own help center.

By Prop Desk Staff · Read from MFFU's help center Nov 4, 2026

The short version

  • Rapid and Pro evaluations use a 50% consistency rule; Rapid EOD uses 30%. Builder has no consistency rule in the evaluation, but a 50% rule on every funded payout.
  • Funded Rapid and Rapid EOD accounts pay daily at a 90/10 split once your balance clears a buffer ($2,100 on a 50K), with a $500 minimum.
  • Builder pays 80/20: 2 qualifying days and $500 of new profit per cycle, up to $2,000 per payout on a 50K, for up to 5 sim payouts before live.
  • Pro pays 80/20 every 14 calendar days, with a $1,000 minimum above the buffer. After the first payout the drawdown moves to start + $100 and stops there.
  • Funded Rapid accounts don't allow Tier 1 news trading, and none of the funded Rapid, Rapid EOD or Pro accounts has a consistency rule.

Covers MFFU's Rapid, Rapid EOD, Builder and Pro plans; the Flex and Studio plans aren't covered here. Figures are for a 50K account unless a table lists other sizes.

  • Rapid: end-of-day max loss in the evaluation, no daily loss limit, up to 5 minis (50 micros). Tier 1 news trading is allowed in the evaluation.
  • Rapid EOD: no daily loss limit, up to 3 minis (30 micros), and a 7-day inactivity rule.
  • Builder: end-of-day trailing max loss of $2,000, or $1,500 with the add-on (*). A $1,000 daily loss limit works as a soft pause: trading stops for the day, the account isn't failed. Up to 4 minis (40 micros).
  • Pro: no daily loss limit. 100K: $6,000 target, $3,000 max loss. 150K: $9,000 target, $4,500 max loss. The Pro One Day 50K option has a $4,000 target, no consistency rule and a 1-day minimum.

The consistency rule

No single day's profit can be more than your plan's share of your total profit. MFFU's own math for a 50K with a $3,000 target and the 50% rule: your biggest day can be up to $1,500 if you want to pass right at the target.

Going over doesn't fail you. You keep trading more days until your biggest day fits under the line.

Rapid and Rapid EOD payouts: daily above the buffer

SizeBufferMinimum payout
25K$1,100$500
50K$2,100$500
100K$3,100$500
150K$4,600$500
  • You can withdraw profit above starting balance + buffer on any day. On Rapid, the first payout can come 24 hours after your first funded trade.
  • 90/10 split (MFFU moved Rapid to 90/10 on Jan 12, 2026). No consistency rule and no daily loss limit once funded.
  • Rapid funded drawdown: $2,000 intraday trailing from your equity high (50K), locking at $100 above your starting balance. Rapid EOD funded drawdown: $2,000 end-of-day trailing, also locking at $100 above start. Tier 1 news trading isn't allowed once funded.
  • Rapid EOD lists no cap per payout cycle.
  • Rapid moves to a live account automatically after $10,000 net profit in a single day, or by risk team review.

Builder payouts: 80/20, capped per cycle

  • 80/20 split. The 50% consistency rule applies to each payout cycle and resets after each approved payout.
  • Each cycle needs 2 qualifying days. Your first payout needs $500 of net profit above the buffer; later payouts need $500 of profit since the last one.
  • 50K: $500 minimum, $2,000 maximum per payout, above a $2,100 buffer ($1,600 if you chose the $1,500 max loss).
  • The $1,000 daily loss limit (soft pause) continues on the funded account.
  • Up to 5 sim payouts, then you move to live. On Builder live: daily payouts with a $250 minimum, no consistency rule, the same $1,000 daily loss limit, one live account at a time, and a 21-day cooldown if a live account is breached.

Other Builder sizes: 25K pays $250 to $1,000 per payout. 100K and 150K have $1,000 and $1,500 minimums, with $3,100 and $4,600 buffers.

Pro payouts: every 14 days

SizeTargetMax loss (EOD)Buffer
50K$3,000$2,000$2,100
100K$6,000$3,000$3,100

  • 80/20 split, no daily loss limit, no activation fee, no consistency rule once funded. No Tier 1 news trading on the funded account.
  • One payout every 14 calendar days, counted from your first trade, with a $1,000 minimum above the buffer. Pro payouts are capped at $100,000 per trader.
  • After the first payout the max loss moves to starting balance + $100 and stays there.
  • You're reviewed for live after 3 payouts in a row, at the $100,000 cap (profit above it is forfeited), at a $20,000 profit review, or by invitation from MFFU's risk team. A breached live account means a 21-day cooldown.
  • MFFU also lists The Reserve, a bonus on live accounts tied to 20 profitable live days and gross live payouts of $10K, $20K and $30K.

MFFU's older payout overview says up to 60% can be withdrawn before the buffer clears; its newer Pro article doesn't say this. Check your dashboard before counting on it.

Questions about MFFU rules

What is MFFU's consistency rule?

30% on Rapid EOD evaluations and 50% on Rapid and Pro evaluations: no single day can be more than that share of your total profit. Builder has no consistency rule in the evaluation, but a 50% rule on each funded payout. The Pro One Day option has none.

Does MFFU have a consistency rule on funded accounts?

Only on Builder, where your biggest day can't be more than 50% of the cycle's profit when you request a payout. It resets after each approved payout. Funded Rapid, Rapid EOD and Pro accounts have no consistency rule.

What is MFFU's profit split?

90/10 on Rapid and Rapid EOD funded accounts (since Jan 12, 2026) and 80/20 on Builder and Pro.

How often does MFFU pay out?

Rapid and Rapid EOD: any day your balance is above the buffer. Builder: each cycle of 2 qualifying days and $500 of new profit. Pro: every 14 calendar days from your first trade.

What is MFFU's payout buffer?

$1,100 on a 25K, $2,100 on a 50K, $3,100 on a 100K and $4,600 on a 150K. A Builder 50K with the $1,500 max loss uses $1,600.

What is MFFU's minimum payout?

$500 on Rapid, Rapid EOD and Builder 50K. $1,000 on Pro. Builder 25K is $250, Builder 100K $1,000 and Builder 150K $1,500.

When does MFFU's drawdown stop trailing?

On Rapid, Rapid EOD and Builder it locks at $100 above your starting balance once your balance gets there. On Pro it moves to starting balance + $100 after your first payout and stays there.

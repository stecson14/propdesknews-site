The short version

  • The Test has a 50% consistency rule: your best day must be under 50% of your net profit, over at least 3 trading days. Missing it doesn't fail you; your profit goal just moves up.
  • PRO accounts pay an 80/20 split, and you can withdraw from day one once you've built the buffer (starting balance plus max drawdown, e.g. $52,000 on 50K).
  • There's no consistency rule and no daily loss limit in the funded stage, and Take Profit Trader says there's no cap on the withdrawal amount.
  • The Test and PRO+ use end-of-day trailing drawdown. PRO uses intraday trailing drawdown.
  • PRO+ is invite-only, pays 90/10 and has no buffer requirement.

The 50% consistency rule (Test only)

In one line: Best day under 50% of net profit over at least 3 trading days. Missing it raises your goal; it doesn’t fail you. None on PRO.

Your best day divided by your net profit must be below 50%, and you need at least 3 trading days (any day with one trade counts). If your best day is too big, you haven't failed. Your goal just becomes more than 2× your best day, and it shows in your dashboard.

Take Profit Trader's example (50K, $3,000 target): +$2,000, +$600, +$500. The best day is $2,000 ÷ $3,100 = 65%, which is too high, so you need more than $4,000 in total.

There is no consistency rule once you reach PRO.

PRO payouts and the buffer

In one line: 80/20 from day one once your balance reaches the buffer ($52,000 on a 50K), then payouts can be daily with no stated cap.

PRO pays 80/20. You can withdraw from day one, once your balance reaches the buffer, which equals your starting balance plus your max drawdown.

After that, payouts can be daily, with no minimum number of profit days, and Take Profit Trader says there's no cap on the amount and withdrawals are usually processed the same day.

PRO account rules

In one line: Intraday trailing drawdown, hand-placed trades only, one trading day a week, and flat 1 minute either side of FOMC, NFP and CPI.

  • Intraday trailing drawdown, the same amount as your Test. It follows your peak balance including open profit and stops once it reaches your starting balance. Touch it and the account is liquidated.
  • No bots or algos. Every trade has to be placed by hand.
  • Trade at least one day per calendar week (Sunday to Friday) to keep the account active. Contact support if you need an exception.
  • No counter positions: you can't hold opposite positions in the same or closely related products. This applies to Test, PRO and PRO+.
  • Be flat around big news: no open positions or orders from one minute before to one minute after FOMC statements, NFP and CPI.
  • Exit before limit up or limit down. If a price limit is hit while you're in a position, you lose the account.

PRO+

In one line: Invite-only live account: 90/10, end-of-day trailing drawdown and no buffer.

PRO+ is a live-market account that Take Profit Trader offers by invitation to its most consistent PRO traders. It pays 90/10, uses end-of-day trailing drawdown and has no buffer requirement. The weekly trading, news and counter-position rules still apply.

Questions about Take Profit Trader rules

What is Take Profit Trader's consistency rule?

In the Test, your best day must be below 50% of your net profit, over at least 3 trading days. If it isn't, you haven't failed; your profit goal becomes more than twice your best day.

Is there a consistency rule in the Take Profit Trader PRO account?

No. Take Profit Trader says it does not impose a consistency rule in the funded stage.

When can I withdraw from a Take Profit Trader PRO account?

From day one, once your balance reaches the buffer: your starting balance plus your max drawdown, for example $52,000 on a 50K account. After that you can withdraw daily.

What is Take Profit Trader's profit split?

80/20 in PRO accounts and 90/10 in PRO+ accounts, which are invite-only.

Does Take Profit Trader have a daily loss limit?

No. Take Profit Trader says it has no daily loss limit, no funded-stage consistency rule and no scaling plan.

Can I use a trading bot at Take Profit Trader?

Not in a PRO account. Its PRO rules say every trade must be placed manually and no automated or bot trading is allowed.

Futures firms

"Monthly" means the evaluation renews each month until you pass. Check the drawdown type before you compare prices: a cheap eval with a tight trailing drawdown can cost more in resets.

CFD / forex firms

Drawdown here is usually a percent of the starting balance. FTMO prices in EUR and Lux in GBP; everything else is USD unless shown.

Perps firms

Perpetual futures (perps) have no expiry and trade 24/7. Funding payments and trading fees can count against your drawdown, so read each firm's rules before you compare prices. Most perps accounts are simulated, priced from exchanges like Hyperliquid, Bybit or Kraken.

Topstep payout rules and consistency rule

How Topstep pays you, and the two consistency rules that decide when. Every number here is from Topstep's own help center.

By Prop Desk Staff · Read from Topstep's help center Nov 5, 2026

The short version

  • You keep 90% of profits. The minimum payout is $125. Traders who joined the new Topstep dashboard before Jan 12, 2026 keep 100% of their first $10,000.
  • In the Express Funded Account you pick a payout path: Standard needs 5 winning days of $150 or more, and Consistency needs 3 trading days with your best day at or under 40% of profit.
  • Each Express Funded payout is up to 50% of your balance, capped at $2,000 to $6,000 depending on size and path. The cap doubles if you added a Daily Loss Limit when you bought the Combine.
  • In the Trading Combine, your best day must stay at or under 55% of your total profit, or your profit target goes up.
  • Live Funded payouts have no cap, and you can take a payout every day once you have 30 winning days in the Live account.
  • New Labs drops #010 and #011: a $50K Daily Payouts+ Combine at $119 (EOD) or $149 (static), with daily payouts of $250 to $1,500 after a $2,000 buffer.

Express Funded payout paths

When you activate an Express Funded Account (XFA), you choose one of two payout paths. Both pay 90/10.

PathTo request a payoutEach payout
Standard5 winning days of $150+ net P&L (they don't need to be in a row), and net profit since your last payout. Your first payout is exempt from the profit check.50% of balance, up to the cap
Consistency3 trading days with at least 1 trade each, and your largest day at or under 40% of total net profit.50% of balance, up to the cap

A day locks in at 4:00 PM CT. The day you request a payout doesn't count toward the next cycle. After each payout your Maximum Loss Limit moves to $0 for good, and the day count starts again.

The two consistency rules

Trading Combine: 55%. Your best day divided by your total profit must be at or under 55%. Go over it and your profit target rises to best day ÷ 0.55. A losing day doesn't reset your best day, and each day locks at 3:10 PM CT. You can pass in as few as 2 days.

SizeProfit targetKeep your best day under
50K$3,000$1,650
100K$6,000$3,300
150K$9,000$4,950

Example from Topstep: on a 50K Combine, an $1,800 best day ÷ 0.55 makes your target $3,273, so you need $3,273 without any day bigger than $1,800. Extra profit made on the same day just raises the best day, so the rest has to come on a later day.

Express Funded, Consistency path: 40%. Your largest day divided by your total net profit must be 40% or less to request a payout, and it isn't rounded. Topstep's example: $3,600 ÷ $9,500 = 37.9%, which qualifies. The count resets after each payout and starts again the trading day after your request.

Live Funded Account

  • 5 winning days of $150+ net P&L per payout cycle (not consecutive).
  • Request up to 50% of your balance, with no dollar cap.
  • Daily payouts unlock after 30 winning days of $150+ in the Live account. You can then request any amount of your unlocked balance once a day (minimum $125). Winning days from the XFA don't count toward the 30.
  • Taking a 100% payout closes the Live account, because the balance reaches the Maximum Loss Limit.

Topstep scaling plan: how many contracts you can trade

Topstep's Scaling Plan is an Express Funded Account (XFA) rule. It sets your maximum position size, the most contracts you can hold at once, from your current account balance. Your XFA starts at a $0 balance, and as the balance grows, so does the number of contracts you can trade.

  • No mid-session increases. If you cross a threshold during the session, the extra contracts unlock at the next session.
  • Micros count 10 to 1. Limits are in mini-contract equivalents: on a $50K XFA with a 2-lot limit you can hold 2 minis, 20 micros or any mix equal to 2 minis. Micro Silver, Micro Bitcoin and Micro Ether are weighted differently.
  • Quick fixes are ignored. Going over and correcting it in under 10 seconds is ignored; staying over for 10 seconds or more can trigger a review.
  • Not on Live. The Live Funded Account uses Dynamic Live Risk Expansion instead.

Your current limit shows on the TopstepX Risk Settings page. Topstep may tighten limits on some products during extreme volatility.

Topstep activation fee

Topstep charges its activation fee once per Express Funded Account you earn, and the amount depends on the Trading Combine path you bought:

  • Standard Path: $149 one-time activation fee per XFA, in exchange for a lower monthly Combine price.
  • No Activation Fee Path: $0 to activate.

Reactivating a lost XFA costs $599 (50K), $699 (100K) or $829 (150K). Topstep doesn't refund an activation on the wrong account, so check which XFA you're activating.

Questions about Topstep rules

What are Topstep's payout rules?

Topstep pays a 90/10 split with a $125 minimum. In the Express Funded Account you need 5 winning days of $150 or more (Standard path) or 3 trading days with your best day at or under 40% of profit (Consistency path). Each payout is up to 50% of your balance, capped by account size. Live Funded payouts have no cap.

What is the Topstep consistency rule?

There are two. In the Trading Combine your best day must stay at or under 55% of your total profit, or your profit target goes up to best day divided by 0.55. On the Express Funded Consistency path your largest day must be 40% or less of your total net profit to request a payout.

How much can I withdraw from a Topstep Express Funded Account?

Up to 50% of your balance per payout, capped at $2,000, $3,000 or $5,000 on the Standard path and $3,000, $4,000 or $6,000 on the Consistency path for 50K, 100K and 150K accounts. The caps double if you added a Daily Loss Limit when you bought the Combine.

How long do Topstep payouts take?

Prop-to-brokerage is same day if requested by 12 PM CT, Aeropay is instant after approval, Wise and ACH take 1 to 3 business days, and wires take 5 to 10. Topstep says approval itself can take 1 to 3 business days.

Does Topstep charge payout fees?

ACH and wire payouts cost $30 each. Prop-to-brokerage has no fee, and Topstep charges no fee for Aeropay or Wise, though your bank may.

When do Topstep daily payouts unlock?

After 30 winning days of $150 or more in the Live Funded Account. Winning days from the Express Funded Account don't count.

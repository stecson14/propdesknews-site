The short version

  • 30% consistency applies only on Fundamental and Premium CASH (funded) accounts: no day above 30% of total profit, measured over at least 4 days. None on the evaluations and none on Express to Live.
  • Fundamental and Premium use an EOD trailing drawdown that stops trailing at $50,100 (50K) or $100,100 (100K) on funded accounts. Express to Live uses a static drawdown.
  • Fundamental pays 80/20 every 10 trading days; Premium pays every 5 trading days with a split that rises from 75% to 100% over 5 payouts.
  • Premium allows overnight and weekend holds; Fundamental and Express to Live must be flat by 9:59 PM UTC.
  • Express to Live has no minimum days and no daily loss limit; hitting the CASH target and clicking Switch to Live pays a fixed bonus and moves you to LIVE.

The Phidias 30% consistency rule

In one line: Fundamental and Premium funded accounts: no day above 30% of total profit, over at least 4 days. None on evaluations, LIVE or Express to Live.

On Fundamental and Premium funded (CASH) accounts, no single day can be more than 30% of your total profit. Phidias' own example: with $1,000 total profit, your best day can be $300. Make $330 in a day and you need $1,100 total before you qualify.

The rule is measured over at least 4 trading days. There is no consistency rule on the Fundamental or Premium evaluations, on LIVE accounts, or anywhere on Express to Live.

Express to Live (E2L) rules

In one line: Static drawdown, no minimum days, no consistency and no daily loss limit. Reach the CASH target and Switch to Live for a fixed bonus.

Express to Live comes in 25K, 50K, 100K and 150K. The evaluation has a static drawdown, no minimum days, no consistency rule and no daily loss limit, and news trading is allowed. Pass and the account becomes a funded CASH account with the same rules.

When you reach the CASH target, the Switch to Live button pays a fixed bonus to your Phidias Wallet (at an 80/20 split), credits your new LIVE account and converts the account. No withdrawal request or minimum days are needed.

E2L accounts count toward a 5-account limit. Like Fundamental, E2L positions must be closed before the end of the trading day: no overnight or weekend holds.

Activation fees and holding positions

In one line: Subscription accounts pay a $149 activation fee on a 50K; one-time-payment accounts pay none. Only Premium can hold overnight.

  • Activation fee: on subscription accounts, $149 on 50K and 100K and $169 on 150K for Fundamental and Premium. Phidias' table lists $83 (25K), $149 (50K and 100K) and $169 (150K) for subscription Express to Live accounts. One-time-payment accounts pay none. Check the total at checkout.
  • Overnight and weekend: allowed on Premium only; Fundamental and Express to Live must close everything by 9:59 PM UTC or the account is disqualified.
  • Premium Cash Account Reset: $399 (50K), $499 (100K) or $599 (150K), once per account, and only within 24 hours of the liquidation.

Questions about Phidias Propfirm rules

What is the Phidias consistency rule?

On Fundamental and Premium funded (CASH) accounts, no single day can exceed 30% of your total profit, measured over at least 4 trading days. There is none on the evaluations, on LIVE, or on Express to Live.

What drawdown does Phidias use?

Fundamental and Premium use an end-of-day trailing drawdown ($2,500 on 50K, $3,000 on 100K, $4,500 on 150K) that stops trailing at $50,100 or $100,100 on funded accounts. Express to Live uses a static drawdown that never trails.

What is Phidias' profit split?

Fundamental pays 80/20. Premium starts at 75% and rises to 100% over 5 payouts. Express to Live bonus payouts are paid at 80/20.

Can you hold overnight at Phidias?

Only on Premium accounts, including over the weekend. Fundamental and Express to Live accounts must close all positions by 9:59 PM UTC.

How often does Phidias pay?

Every 10 trading days on Fundamental and every 5 on Premium, with a $500 minimum, through Rise. Express to Live pays a fixed bonus when you switch to LIVE.

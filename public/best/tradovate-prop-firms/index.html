Is Tradovate free with a prop account?

At the firms we read, Tradovate comes with the account. Phidias says Tradovate, Rithmic and DeepCharts are "all included at no extra cost", and Tradeify prices its Tradovate, Rithmic and WealthCharts accounts the same (Phidias, Tradeify).

Two things can cost you. On first login you sign the Non-Professional Data Agreement, or your market data is delayed. Level 2 depth and EUREX data are paid add-ons bought from Tradovate (Tradeify).

How to connect Tradovate to your prop account

  1. Pick Tradovate at checkout. Apex and Tradeify say you can't switch an account to another broker later; you buy a new one (Apex, Tradeify).
  2. Sign in to Tradovate with the credentials from your firm's dashboard (MFFU login steps).
  3. Review and sign the agreement, then choose Simulation. Top One Futures calls it "Tradovate prop - Simulation" (Top One Futures).
  4. If you bought several accounts, pick the right one in the account list before you trade (MFFU).
  5. Want TradingView charts? Link the same login inside TradingView: our TradingView guide.

Tradovate vs the alternatives

Cloud futures platform (web, desktop and mobile) owned by NinjaTrader Group. Most futures firms give you a Tradovate login, and the same login opens TradingView and NinjaTrader.

  • NinjaTrader: Desktop futures platform with deep charting and automation. At most prop firms it connects with your Tradovate login; a few run it on Rithmic. NinjaTrader prop firms
  • Rithmic: A data feed and order route, not a single app. A Rithmic login opens R|Trader Pro plus platforms such as Quantower, Sierra Chart, ATAS, Bookmap and MotiveWave. Rithmic prop firms
  • TradingView: Charting site most traders already use. Prop firms connect it through Tradovate, Plus500 or a built-in link. TradingView prop firms

Questions

Does Apex use Tradovate?

Yes. Apex offers Tradovate, Rithmic and WealthCharts accounts, picked at signup, and each one is its own account.

Does Topstep use Tradovate?

No. Topstep says TopstepX is the only available trading platform.

Is Tradovate free with a prop firm account?

At the firms we read, yes. Phidias includes Tradovate at no extra cost and Tradeify charges the same for every platform. Level 2 and EUREX data are paid add-ons from Tradovate.

Can I use NinjaTrader with a Tradovate prop account?

Yes at most of these firms. Tradeify says NinjaTrader connects with your Tradovate credentials after you log in to Tradovate once and sign the data agreement.

Is MT5 free with a prop account?

MT5 itself comes with the account at the firms we read. Expert Advisors can cost extra: FundedNext says automated trading is only permitted on MetaTrader and an EA usage fee applies (FundedNext).

Where you live matters. Blue Guardian says US clients are restricted to Match-Trader and TradeLocker (Blue Guardian).

How to connect MT5 to your prop account

  1. Open MT5 on the web, Windows, Mac, iOS or Android (FundingPips).
  2. Search the firm's name in the server list and pick the server shown in your dashboard (FundingPips).
  3. Log in with the account number and password from your dashboard (FTMO MT5 login).

MT5 vs the alternatives

The standard CFD and forex platform, with Expert Advisors for automation. Most CFD prop firms offer it.

  • TradeLocker: Browser CFD platform with TradingView charts built in, common at newer CFD firms. TradeLocker prop firms
  • TradingView: Charting site most traders already use. Prop firms connect it through Tradovate, Plus500 or a built-in link. TradingView prop firms

Questions

Which prop firms use MT5?

On our Nov 11, 2026 check, the CFD firms here list MT5 on their own sites, including FTMO, FundedNext, FundingPips and Blue Guardian.

Can US traders use MT5 at a prop firm?

Often not. Blue Guardian says US clients are restricted to Match-Trader and TradeLocker, and FundingPips points US and Canada residents to Match-Trader. Check your firm's platform page for your country.

Can I use Expert Advisors on MT5 at a prop firm?

At some firms. FundedNext allows automated trading only on MetaTrader and charges an EA usage fee.

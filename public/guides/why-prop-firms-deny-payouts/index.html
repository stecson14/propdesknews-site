Why prop firms deny payouts

Most denied payouts come from a written rule the trader missed: consistency, minimum trading days, the payout buffer, news trading, copy trading or KYC. Each rule below is quoted from the firms' own pages, so you can check yours before you request.

By Prop Desk Staff · Sources listed at the end

The short version

  • A denied payout almost always traces back to a written rule, split across the firm's help center, terms and FAQ.
  • Missing a consistency or day-count rule usually just delays a payout. Prohibited conduct can close the account.
  • Check your dashboard against each rule before you request, and ask for the exact rule in writing if denied.

Every rule here is from the firm's own help center or terms, read on the date shown next to it (all 2026). The firm's dashboard and its own page are the final word.

Consistency rule broken

The rule. Your biggest day can't be more than a set share of your total profit when you request.

Firms with this rule

  • Topstep (Consistency path): your largest day must be at or under 40% of total net profit. Read Nov 5.
  • Phidias: no single day over 30% of total profit on funded CASH accounts. Read Nov 9.
  • FTMO: best day at most 50% on 1-Step and 30% on Instant; going over isn't a breach, you keep trading until it passes. Read Nov 4.

How to check before you request. Put your best day and total profit into our consistency calculator, or pick from firms with no consistency rule.

Not enough trading or winning days

The rule. Each payout needs a set number of days, often each with a minimum profit.

Firms with this rule

  • Topstep: 5 winning days of $150 or more on the Standard path. Read Nov 5.
  • Lucid: each LucidFlex cycle needs 5 days at or above the profit-day minimum ($150 on a 50K). Read Nov 3.
  • Elite Trader Funding: an Active Trade Day needs $250 of realized profit and at least 30% of your best day. Read Nov 4.

How to check before you request. Count only days that meet the minimum. Timing: how long to pass a prop firm.

Below the payout buffer or minimum balance

The rule. Only profit above a set balance can be withdrawn.

Firms with this rule

  • MyFundedFutures: Rapid pays profit above starting balance plus a buffer ($2,100 on a 50K), with a $500 minimum. Read Nov 4.
  • Take Profit Trader: on PRO accounts you can withdraw once the balance reaches the buffer, starting balance plus max drawdown ($52,000 on a 50K). Read Nov 6.
  • Purdia: a Protected Buffer that can't be paid out, for customers whose first purchase is on or after Oct 12, 2026. Read Nov 4.

How to check before you request. Balance minus the buffer is the most you can request. See the floor move in the Drawdown Lab.

News trading during restricted times

The rule. Some firms ban orders around major releases, or don't count profit made then.

Firms with this rule

  • Take Profit Trader: be flat on PRO accounts from 1 minute before to 1 minute after FOMC statements, NFP and CPI. Read Nov 6.
  • MyFundedFutures: flat with no orders from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after Tier 1 data. Read Nov 6.
  • FundedNext: on funded CFD accounts, only 40% of profit from trades 5 minutes either side of listed news counts. Read Nov 6.

How to check before you request. Check our economic calendar and the news trading rules at every firm.

Copy trading, hedging or sharing accounts

The rule. No trading with or for others, no shared devices, no hedging across accounts.

Firms with this rule

  • Topstep: bans "Coordinated trading" and "Trading on behalf of others." Read Nov 11.
  • Apex: "Sharing MAC addresses, computers, IPs, credit cards, or trade copying with other traders is strictly forbidden," and breakers forfeit their accounts and balances. Read Nov 11.
  • Lucid: hedging across accounts is prohibited, and a hedging violation costs intraday payout eligibility. Read Nov 6.

How to check before you request. A copier should only copy your own accounts, within the limit. See hedging rules and account limits.

KYC or country problems

The rule. Your ID check must pass, and some countries are excluded.

Firms with this rule

  • Tradeify: its own Sumsub check plus a separate Rise payout check before your first payout. Read Nov 6.
  • Alpha Futures: Veriff ID check required before a Qualified account or any payout. Read Nov 6.
  • Topstep: some countries can hold an Express Funded Account but not a Live Funded Account. Read Nov 6.

How to check before you request. Finish KYC early, with a payout account in your own name. See restricted countries and KYC rules.

Gambling-style trading (max position and lot size rules)

The rule. Firms ban trades that look like a bet, and each draws the line differently.

Firms with this rule

  • Topstep: trading your full max position size into a scheduled major release is a prohibited strategy. Read Nov 6.
  • Apex: tiny targets with huge stops are prohibited, with Apex's example of a 5-tick target and a 150-tick stop. Read Nov 6.
  • FXIFY: "high leverage news trading" is prohibited. Read Nov 6.

How to check before you request. If one big trade made most of the profit, read the firm's prohibited strategies page first.

Using a disallowed platform, EA or VPN

The rule. Approved software only, and at some firms no bots or VPN.

Firms with this rule

  • Apex: no AI, autobots, algorithms or HFT; using them forfeits the balance. Read Nov 6.
  • Topstep: "VPNs, proxy services, TOR, geo-location obfuscation, and other identity-masking services are not permitted." Read Nov 11.
  • FTMO: algorithmic trading and EAs are allowed if legitimate and "replicable on a live account." Read Nov 6.

How to check before you request. Turn off any VPN, and check the bots rule before an EA. See automated trading prop firms.

Requesting outside the payout window or above the cap

The rule. Requests have a schedule, a minimum and a cap.

Firms with this rule

  • Topstep: each Express Funded payout is up to 50% of the balance, capped at $2,000 to $6,000 by size and path. Read Nov 5.
  • Apex: caps step up over six payouts, $1,500 to $3,000 on a 50K EOD account. Read Nov 3.
  • The5ers: one payout request per 14 calendar days. Read Nov 6.

How to check before you request. Check your last payout date and your cap. Or see no payout cap prop firms and daily payout futures firms.

Do prop firms ban profitable traders?

Firms reserve the right to review any account, and their terms say so; being profitable isn't listed as a reason. Topstep: "Our Internal Teams routinely review all Topstep profiles for compliance with the Topstep Terms of Use and program integrity." During a hold you can't request payouts. Outcomes run from a warning to "Delay or denial of a Payout request" and "Permanent account closure."

Apex: "Apex reserves the right to audit accounts further if there is a suspicion of non-compliance." That's the clause; we don't add anecdotes. The graveyard covers what happens when firms stop paying altogether.

Funded account "hidden" rules: where to find them

The rules are written down, but split across the help center, the terms and the FAQ. Where the big firms keep the payout-blocking ones:

  • Topstep: the help center's Prohibited Conduct and Economic Releases articles, plus the payout policy.
  • Apex: the help center's Prohibited Activities page and the payout article for your account type.
  • MyFundedFutures: Fair Play and Prohibited Trading Practices, plus the news trading policy.
  • FTMO: the FAQ, the Forbidden Trading Practices page and the terms and conditions.

Our firm rules pages pull these into one place with a Rule Scanner; start from rules compared, and see the November rule change report.

What to do if your payout is denied

  • Ask for the exact rule in writing.
  • Check your dashboard. Best day against total profit, the day count, and your balance against the buffer.
  • Use the firm's support route. At Topstep, a hold lifts or support reaches out within 7 business days.
  • Fix it and request again if it was a consistency, day or buffer rule.
  • Think before a chargeback. A card dispute is about the fee you paid, not the payout, and firms' terms deal with it. This isn't legal advice.

If a firm won't name the rule, or stops paying across the board, see the red flags in are prop firms legit?

Questions

Can a prop firm refuse to pay you?

Yes, when a written rule wasn't met. Topstep lists "Delay or denial of a Payout request" among its actions for prohibited conduct, and Apex says traders who break its prohibited-activity rules forfeit their accounts and balances. Ask which rule, in writing.

How long do prop firm payouts take?

It depends on the firm and method. Topstep lists ACH at 1 to 3 business days and international wire at 5 to 10. FTMO reviews in 1 to 2 business days, then usually pays 1 to 2 business days after the invoice is approved.

Do prop firms deny payouts often?

Firms rarely publish it. Tradeify's homepage claims "0% payout denials", and MyFundedFutures says "75%+ of payouts" are auto-approved in milliseconds (read Nov 6, 2026). Those are the firms' own claims; no firm we track publishes an audited denial rate.

Does a denied payout fail my account?

Not by itself. A missed consistency or day-count rule usually means you keep trading until you qualify; FTMO says going over its consistency limit isn't a breach. Prohibited conduct is different: Topstep and Apex can close the account.

Can I get a refund if a payout is denied?

Usually not: Topstep says "Most fees at Topstep are non-refundable." Some firms return the evaluation fee with the first payout, as FTMO does on the 2-Step, so a denied first payout delays that refund too.

Trade copiers for prop firms: rules and the best copiers

Most futures prop firms let you copy trades between your own accounts, and some even between firms, but almost all ban copying someone else's trades. Check the firm's rule first, then pick a copier that supports your platform.

Edited by Steven T. · Sources listed at the end

The short version

  • 13 of 14 firms we read let you copy between your own accounts; Earn2Trade bans copiers outright.
  • 4 firms say you may copy to or from your own accounts at other firms; Apex prohibits it.
  • Copying someone else's trades, signals or a trade group is banned almost everywhere.
  • Free option: Tradovate Group Trading or the TopstepX Trade Copier. Paid copiers run about $29 to $149 a month on list price.

Every firm rule here is from the firm's own help center or terms, and every copier price from the copier's own pricing page, read Nov 11, 2026. Rules change; the firm's page is the final word.

Copy trading vs trade copiers

A trade copier is software that repeats the orders from one of your accounts (the leader) on your other accounts (the followers). Prop firms judge it as your own trading.

Copy trading usually means following someone else: a signal service, a trade group or another trader's account. That is what firms ban, and they can spot it from IPs, devices and matching fills. How they catch it: how prop firms detect copy trading.

Best trade copier for Tradovate, NinjaTrader and TradingView

Tradovate

Start with Tradovate's own Group Trading: it costs nothing extra, Apex switches it on once you have more than one Tradovate account, and MFFU, Phidias and Elite Trader Funding all name it. For risk limits per account, Tradecopia and Tradesyncer both connect to a Tradovate login in the cloud.

More firms on this platform: Tradovate prop firms.

NinjaTrader

Replikanto and CrossTrade run inside NinjaTrader 8 on your own PC, so the PC has to stay on. Tradecopia and Tradesyncer list NinjaTrader too and run in the cloud. Only the Replikanto compliance edition is approved at Take Profit Trader and Elite.

See NinjaTrader prop firms.

TradingView

Tradecopia, Tradesyncer and Duplikium list TradingView. CrossTrade turns TradingView alerts into NinjaTrader orders, which is automation, so the firm's bot rule applies as well as its copier rule. See TradingView prop firms.

Lucid

Lucid says trade copiers are permitted as long as they follow its rules, and you own any software error. Lucid runs on Tradovate (CQG) and Rithmic, so Tradovate Group Trading or a cloud copier that supports your side both work.

Firms with a built-in copier

6 of the firms we read name a copier you get with the platform:

  • Topstep: TopstepX Trade Copier.
  • Apex: Tradovate Group Trading, on automatically.
  • MFFU: Tradovate group copier.
  • Take Profit Trader: Platform-native tools approved.
  • Elite Trader Funding: Platform built-in copiers approved.
  • Phidias: On Tradovate; none on Rithmic.

Top One Futures also advertises copy trading on up to 20 accounts on its homepage, but only between accounts of the same program and size.

Copier risks that cost accounts

  • The wrong leader. Topstep says the lead account must have the lowest maximum position size in the group, and Alpha Futures says to lead with your smallest account. A bigger leader can push followers over their contract limit.
  • Different drawdowns. Each follower keeps its own loss limits. On TopstepX, when the leader hits its daily loss limit every follower is flattened with it, and a payout request unlinks the followers.
  • Opposite positions. Apex says every Performance Account must trade the same direction, and Take Profit Trader bans offsetting positions. A copier set to reverse trades breaks both. More in hedging rules by firm.
  • Mixing account types. TradeDay bans copying between Funded Live and evaluation or Funded Sim accounts, and Top One Futures only allows the same program and size.
  • Too many accounts. A copier makes it easy to pass the cap; check max accounts by firm first.
  • A banned copier. At Earn2Trade a copier can cost you the account and the withdrawal, with no refund. It is one of the reasons in why prop firms deny payouts.

Questions

Can you use a trade copier with a prop firm?

At most futures firms we read, yes, between accounts you own. Earn2Trade bans copiers on every program, and Take Profit Trader and Elite Trader Funding only allow the copiers on their approved lists (read Nov 11, 2026).

Is copy trading allowed at Apex?

Between your own accounts, yes: Apex allows copy trading across Performance Accounts under your personal and business name, from one lead account you trade by hand, and all of them must trade the same direction. Copying to or from anyone else's account or any third-party account is prohibited.

Can I copy trades between two different prop firms?

Some firms say yes: FundedNext Futures (accounts in the same verified name), Blue Guardian Futures, Top One Futures and TradeDay (into TradeDay). Apex's user agreement prohibits copying to or from third-party accounts, and most others don't say, so ask before you link them.

What is the best free trade copier?

The one built into your platform: Tradovate Group Trading and the TopstepX Trade Copier cost nothing extra. Tradecopia, Tradesyncer, CrossTrade and Replikanto all offer 7-day free trials, and Duplikium starts you with €20 of free credit.

Does a trade copier count as automation?

It depends on the firm. Elite Trader Funding treats its approved copiers as the only authorized automated tools, Take Profit Trader bans bots but approves named copiers, and Apex requires the lead account to be traded by hand with no bots.

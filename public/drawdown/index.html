Static

The line is set once, at start minus the max drawdown, and never moves. A winning streak doesn't shrink your room.

End-of-day trailing

At each close the line moves up to that close minus the max drawdown. What you make and give back inside a day doesn't move it.

Intraday trailing

Every new high, including open profit on a trade you haven't closed, moves the line up. A winner that comes back to break-even still cost you room.

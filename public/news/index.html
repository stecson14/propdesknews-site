Confirmed by the firm

Goat Funded Futures puts every plan on a 1-minute hold rule

New plan or update

Every plan now uses one "1 minute rule" (from Mon Nov 9): at each payout request and at eval pass, at least 70% of closed trades and 70% of net profit must come from trades held longer than 1 minute; otherwise the payout is declined and the under-1-minute profit for that cycle is deducted (not a breach). KYC and AML Policy updated Nov 4: Tradeify Futures residents of India and the United Kingdom (with Brazil, China, Italy, Mexico, Romania, South Korea, Spain, Taiwan, Thailand, UAE) must pass identity verification before they receive a funded account, including Lightning Funded; everyone else before the first payout. The article's FAQ still lists the older country list without India and the UK

Source: help.tradeify.co/en/articles/11384149-kyc-and-aml-policy ("Updated yesterday" on Nov 5; screenshot) · Full story →

KYC and AML Policy, Tradeify Help Center. Before: archived copy (Wayback Machine), Jun 10, 2026. After: Tradeify's live page, read Nov 7, 2026. Quoted word for word.

When is it required?

Before
  • You can complete this verification at any time through your dashboard
  • It is mandatory before requesting your first payout
  • We recommend completing it early to avoid delays when you're ready for payouts
After
  • Tradeify Futures (Growth, Select, Lightning): identity verification is required before your first payout
  • Tradeify Futures, residents of Brazil, China, Italy, Mexico, Romania, South Korea, Spain, Taiwan, Thailand, India, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom and China: identity verification is required before you receive a funded account, including Lightning Funded accounts
  • Tradeify 247 Instant Funding: identity verification is part of your purchase
  • Tradeify 247 evaluations (2-Step, 1-Step, and legacy APE-X): identity verification is required after you pass, before your funded account is activated
  • Crypto purchases require identity verification at the time of purchase, regardless of account type.
  • You can start it at any time from your dashboard — we recommend completing it early to avoid delays

Payout providers

Before
  • Tradeify Crypto uses Rise exclusively for payouts. If Rise doesn't support your country, contact Tradeify support for assistance.
  • If Rise is not available in your country, Tradeify will use Plane as an alternative payout provider.
After
  • Rise is Tradeify's default payout provider.
  • Tradeify Futures: if Rise is not available in your country, Tradeify will use Plane as an alternative payout provider. Tradeify 247 also offers crypto payouts through Confirmo.

What drawdown do MFFU and Topstep use?

MFFU: Intraday trailing (Rapid) or EOD (Rapid EOD); 50K target $3,000. Topstep: Max loss limit; optional daily loss limit raises payout caps.

Do MFFU and Topstep have promo codes?

MFFU: code CLUB (40% off). Topstep: no firm-published code. Checked Thu Nov 12, 2026.

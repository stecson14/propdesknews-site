How it works

Pick the contract, type how many dollars you are willing to lose on the trade, and set your stop in points or ticks.

The result is the most contracts that keep the loss at your stop under your risk, with the dollar risk per contract. If one contract already risks too much, trade the micro or tighten the stop.

Questions

How many MNQ contracts can I trade with a $300 risk and a 20-point stop?

MNQ is $0.50 a tick and 4 ticks a point, so $2 a point. A 20-point stop risks $40 per contract, so $300 ÷ $40 = 7.5, rounded down to 7 contracts.

How do micros compare to minis?

A micro is one tenth of the mini. MES ticks at $1.25 against $12.50 for ES, and MNQ at $0.50 against $5.00 for NQ.

Does the firm’s contract limit override this?

Yes. Each account size has a maximum number of contracts, and many firms count micros as a fraction of a mini. Use the smaller of this answer and the firm’s limit.

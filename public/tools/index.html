True cost of an eval

What you actually pay to reach a first payout attempt: eval, renewals, resets and activation.

Reset and activation fees are on each firm's own page; our profiles list them where the firm states them. Codes often don't apply to resets.

Contracts per trade

How many contracts you can hold for a given stop without betting more than you planned.

Check the firm's contract limit for your account size; micros usually count as a fraction of a mini.

Drawdown runway

How many bad days the account can take, and how much you must make to pass.

Trailing drawdowns move the line up as you profit, so the runway shrinks after a good day. Watch the three types move in the Drawdown Lab, or read the glossary.

Days to first payout

At your pace, how long until you can ask for money.

Add the firm's payout cycle (weekly, every 14 days) on top. A consistency rule can hold a payout even when the numbers work.

Consistency rule

Does your best day break the firm's consistency rule, and how much more profit do you need?

Open the consistency calculator →

Pass-odds simulator

How often would your win rate, average win and average loss pass a plan's target before its drawdown?

Open the pass-odds simulator →

Plays your numbers through 1,000 evaluations with a static, end-of-day or intraday trailing drawdown. A simulation of your inputs, not a firm's pass rate.

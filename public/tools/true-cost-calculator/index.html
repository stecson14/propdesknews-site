Questions

How do I calculate the true cost of a prop firm challenge?

Add the eval price, one renewal for every extra month on a monthly plan, each reset times the reset fee, and the activation fee. A $100 monthly eval that takes three months, with one $80 reset and a $130 activation fee, costs $100 + $200 + $80 + $130 = $510.

Do discount codes apply to resets and activation fees?

Often not. Most codes take money off the eval itself. Check the firm’s own checkout and help center before you count on a discount for resets or activation.

Is a monthly or a one-time eval cheaper?

It depends on how long you take. A monthly plan is cheaper if you pass in the first month, and every extra month adds another full payment. A one-time fee stays the same however long you take.

How it works

Pick a plan to load its payout rules, or type your own: the qualifying-day minimum, how many qualifying days it needs and the profit you need before you can request. Then set how many days a week you win and your average winning and losing day.

The calendar starts next Monday and marks each qualifying day, each losing day and the day you can request, and it says whether the money or the qualifying days hold you up. Add the firm’s payout processing time on top.

Questions

How long does it take to get a first payout from a prop firm?

At $250 a day toward a $500 minimum plus a $1,000 buffer, you need 6 trading days of profit. If the firm also needs 5 qualifying days, the money is the slower part, so the answer is 6 days, plus the firm’s payout cycle.

What is a qualifying day?

A day that meets the firm’s minimum profit, for example $150 or more. Days below the minimum don’t count toward the number of days the firm requires before a payout.

Why add a buffer?

Some firms require a balance above the starting balance before a payout, and every payout moves you closer to the drawdown. The buffer is the extra profit you plan to leave in the account.
